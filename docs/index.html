اسأل عن الأنظمة السعودية

Ask a question about Saudi law in plain Arabic. Sanad finds the statute article, answers from it, cites it, and says when the law is not there.

This box searches the 717 articles in your browser by word matching, the weakest search in this project (right article first for 41% of real questions, against 63% for the model's search below). It finds articles; writing an answer needs the language model on a GPU server, so the answers below were generated in advance.

    كيف يجيبHow it answers

    For these examples the search combines word matching (BM25) with meaning-based search (bge-m3), and a reranker (bge-reranker-v2-m3) picks the three best articles from the top 20. Those three go to ALLaM-7B, a language model trained by SDAIA, with one instruction: answer only from these texts, cite the one you used, and if none of them answers the question, say «لا أجد الإجابة في النصوص المتاحة» ("I can't find the answer in the available texts").

    The examples above are real outputs on the 717 statute articles cited in ALARB court cases. With the simpler search used in the experiments (multilingual-e5-base alone), the article that answers the question came back for only three of the six questions whose law is in the collection; with this search, for five. Two questions ask about labour and traffic law, which are not in that collection, so the right answer is that sentence. The three columns are the same model before fine-tuning and after two fine-tuning runs that differ in one thing: the first was trained to answer in a few words, the second to add the sentence of the article its answer rests on, copied word for word.

    The test questions most papers report on were written by a language model reading the passage, so they reuse its words. On those, word matching wins and fine-tuning helps. On the 79 questions real people asked, both turn against you: the fine-tuned retriever finds fewer of the right articles, and a model trained to refuse when the wording does not match refuses when people simply use different words. The same pattern showed up in two different models. The honest test is the human one, and it is small: one question is 1.3 points.

    Before fine-tuning, ALLaM answered almost every question whose law it had not been given, and rarely said where its answer came from. Fine-tuning fixes the citations. How often it should refuse is the hard part: with 12% “not found” examples in training it rarely refuses wrongly but often answers when the law is missing; with 18% it is back to refusing almost half the questions it could answer. Training the dial does not move it smoothly.

    حدود المشروعWhat it is not

    It is not legal advice and not a complete body of Saudi law. The served collection is the 717 articles that commercial court cases cite, mostly from the laws of civil procedure, companies, commercial courts, evidence, bankruptcy and the legal profession. The full code, every number, and the notebooks that produced them are in the repository.

    Built by Read Aloush. Data: ArabLegalEval and Najiz QA (THIQAH and KAUST), ALARB (Apache-2.0). Models: ALLaM-7B-Instruct (SDAIA), multilingual-e5-base.
